Multi-Channel MitM (mc-mitm)

Prerequisites: client connected to a legitimate AP, working CSA attack

The attacker uses two wireless interfaces: rogue_client monitors the real channel and rogue_ap runs a rogue AP on a different channel. CSA beacons and deauth frames move the target client from the real AP to the rogue channel. (check CSA attack for more details) Once the client associates with the rogue AP, traffic is forwarded through the attacker, achieving a transparent MitM position without breaking connectivity.

Success: client associates with the rogue AP (ClientState == GotMitm)
